# Code of conduct

This policy applies to this project's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other maintainer-managed community spaces. It covers participation, not a user's learning performance or choice of coding agent.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence respectfully. Disagreement and correction are welcome; personal attacks are not.
- Make room for different experience levels, backgrounds, identities, and accessibility needs. Do not shame someone for asking a question or not understanding a change.
- Respect privacy. Do not disclose another person's identity, affiliation, messages, or project information without permission.
- Avoid harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and repeated disruption after a request to stop.

## Moderation

Maintainers may ask for a comment to be revised, remove harmful content, limit a thread, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. The response should be proportionate to the behavior. Where safe and practical, maintainers should explain their decision and consider the context. Someone involved in a complaint should not be its only reviewer.

## Reporting and review

There is not yet a verified private conduct-reporting contact. For a non-sensitive concern, a participant may ask for moderation in the relevant thread. For a sensitive concern, request a private contact method without posting identifying details or allegations; wait for that channel before sharing them. Do not use a public issue to expose a reporter or another participant. Platform reporting tools remain available for violations of the platform's own rules.

Participants may ask a maintainer to reconsider a decision through an available private channel. We can't guarantee independent review or response times, especially with a small maintainer team. Before inviting broader community participation, maintainers should set up a private reporting channel and a separate contact for complaints involving a maintainer.

For software vulnerabilities, use the separate [security reporting guidance](SECURITY.md).
