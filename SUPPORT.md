# Support

- **Getting started:** [installation](README.md#install-and-verify), [Codex action picker](docs/codex-usage.md), and the [small demo](docs/demo.md).
- **Usage questions and ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/parkyountaek/own-the-change/discussions).
- **Reproducible bugs:** [open an issue](https://github.com/parkyountaek/own-the-change/issues/new/choose) with host version, plugin version, and synthetic steps.
- **First-use feedback:** use the optional [selection-based feedback form](https://github.com/parkyountaek/own-the-change/issues/new?template=first_use_feedback.yml). Written explanations are optional.
- **Security or private-data exposure:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issues or discussions.

Community support is best-effort, with no guaranteed response time. Keep public questions synthetic and sanitized; see the [canonical privacy guidance](docs/protocol/understanding-protocol.md#privacy-and-safety).
